## Description

Pushup Bro turns pushups into an adventure with Carlos, a baby gorilla who
lost his friends in a storm.

Every pushup counts, with your AirPods' motion sensors or your phone's
camera. Earn bananas, rebuild Carlos's island, dress him up and work out
with his gym buddies.

JUNGLE TREK
Fight through seven stages of the jungle against reptiles that want the
island to themselves. Each enemy's colour tells you which pushup hurts it
most: steady, held at the bottom or quick. Rest between waves, level up
your Power, Vitality, Grip and Agility, and win exclusive lizard gear. Beat
King Komo and he joins your crew.

DAILY QUESTS
A short story and a new pushup challenge every day, at the volcano, the
moonlit beach, the temple of the golden banana and up in the clouds.

TRACK YOUR WAY
- AirPods: the motion of your head counts every pushup.
- Camera: prop your phone up beside you and see a live skeleton over
  yourself, coloured by each pushup, with tips when your hips sag. The video
  never leaves your phone.

ALSO
- A wardrobe full of hats, glasses, outfits, fur colours and mat patterns
- Statistics and streaks
- English, German, Spanish, Japanese and Portuguese
