## Description

Pushup Bro turns pushups into an adventure with Carlos, a baby gorilla who was blown away from his tribe by a storm and washed up on your island.

Every pushup counts, with your AirPods' motion sensors or your phone's camera. Earn bananas, rebuild Carlos's island, dress him up and work out with his gym buddies.

BROS
Add friends with a code or a QR code and race them on today's leaderboard. Send waves, bananas and nudges, get a push when your bros work out, and visit their islands.

JUNGLE TREK
Fight through seven stages of the jungle against reptiles that want the island to themselves. Each enemy's colour tells you which pushup hurts it most: steady, held at the bottom or quick. Level up your Power, Vitality, Grip and Agility, and win exclusive lizard gear. Beat King Komo and he joins your crew.

DAILY QUESTS
A short story and a new pushup challenge every day, at the volcano, the moonlit beach, the temple of the golden banana and up in the clouds.

TRACK YOUR WAY
- AirPods: the motion of your head counts every pushup.
- Camera: prop your phone up beside you and see a live skeleton over yourself, with tips when your hips sag. The video never leaves your phone.

YOUR STATS
Streaks, personal bests, your fastest pace, your activity over the week, month and year, and when you like to train.
