Finished music from Suno, not in the app yet. This folder isn't listed in
pubspec.yaml, so nothing here is bundled or played, and the build size doesn't
change. To use a track, move it to assets/audio/music/ and add it to MusicTrack
in lib/core/audio/game_audio.dart.

The files are Suno's MP3s as downloaded, untrimmed (about 200 kbps). Together
they would add about 56 MB to the app. See docs/music_prompts.md for the
prompts they were made from.
